1. Overview

Briven ("the App") is a manual, structural budgeting app for iOS — no bank connections, no CSV/OFX import, no accounts. You enter your income sources, subscriptions, and expenses yourself, and Briven turns that into a clear monthly picture.

Briven is a Privacy-First app. Your financial data is stored on your device and, optionally, synced only to your own private Apple iCloud. We run no backend server and cannot see your income, expenses, or subscriptions. Anonymous, aggregated usage analytics help us improve the app — never your financial data.

By installing or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

3. Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics

Briven uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) to understand how the app is used and to diagnose crashes.

These tools collect only anonymous, aggregated data such as:

  • App open frequency and session duration
  • Feature usage events (e.g., adding an expense, opening Reports)
  • Device type, OS version, and app version
  • Crash logs and stack traces
  • Country/region (derived from IP, not GPS)
Firebase never receives your income, expenses, subscriptions, categories, or any other financial data you enter — that data never leaves your device or your own iCloud. Analytics data is used solely to improve app stability and feature design, and is never sold or used for advertising.

For details on how Firebase handles data, please refer to the Firebase Privacy Policy.

💳

4. In-App Purchase (Apple)

Briven offers an optional, one-time purchase called Briven Lifetime — not a subscription. The purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store.

We never receive your name, email address, or payment details. The App checks whether you own the purchase using Apple's StoreKit framework on your device, and restoring a purchase also goes through Apple. No third-party purchase service is involved.

5. How We Use Information

Collected information is used solely for the following purposes.

  • App Services: Tracking your income, subscriptions, expenses, and savings goals, and turning them into monthly summaries and reports
  • Multi-Device Sync: Synchronizing your financial data across your own Apple devices via iCloud
  • App Lock: Verifying Face ID/Touch ID locally to protect access to the app
  • Purchases: Checking and restoring your optional Briven Lifetime purchase through Apple
  • Data Export: Creating a CSV or PDF file on your device only when you tap Export; the file goes only where you choose in the share sheet, and the temporary copy is deleted afterward
  • Widgets & Control Center: They only open the App to a new expense — they do not read or display your financial data
  • Improvement: Understanding feature usage and diagnosing crashes via Firebase to guide development

We do not sell or share your financial data or usage data with any third party for marketing purposes.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Financial Data (Local): Stored on your device and permanently deleted when you uninstall the App.
  • Financial Data (iCloud): If iCloud sync is in use, uninstalling the App from one device does not remove your private iCloud copy — reinstalling restores it. To remove it, delete your entries in the App before uninstalling, or check iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. Because this data lives in your own private iCloud, we cannot access or delete it on your behalf, but we are happy to help by email.

  • Exported Files: Files you share are under your control. The App deletes its temporary copy as soon as the share sheet closes.
  • Analytics Data: Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics retain aggregated, anonymous event and crash data per Google's data retention policy.

We hold no copy of your financial data on any server we control. If you do not use iCloud sync, uninstalling the App is sufficient to completely remove all your data.

10. Security

Your financial data is stored using Apple's SwiftData framework, protected by the device's file system encryption. Exported files are written with iOS data protection and deleted after sharing.

When iCloud sync is enabled, data is transmitted to and stored in Apple's private CloudKit database under Apple's encryption policy, both in transit and at rest. Your data is never transmitted to our servers.

When app lock is enabled, Face ID / Touch ID authentication is handled entirely by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework and the device's Secure Enclave — your biometric data never leaves your device and Briven never has access to it.

1. 개요

Briven(이하 "앱")은 은행 연동, CSV/OFX 가져오기, 계정 없이 사용하는 iOS용 매뉴얼 예산관리 앱입니다. 수입원, 구독, 지출을 직접 입력하면 Briven이 이를 한눈에 보이는 월별 현황으로 정리해 드립니다.

Briven은 프라이버시 우선 앱입니다. 금융 데이터는 기기에 저장되며, 원하실 경우 본인의 비공개 Apple iCloud로만 동기화됩니다. 저희는 별도 서버를 운영하지 않으며 사용자의 수입·지출·구독을 볼 수 없습니다. 익명·집계 사용 통계는 앱 개선에만 쓰이며 금융 데이터는 절대 포함되지 않습니다.

앱을 설치하거나 사용하면 본 개인정보 처리방침에 동의하는 것으로 간주됩니다. 동의하지 않으시면 앱 사용을 중단해 주세요.
