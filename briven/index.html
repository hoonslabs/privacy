1. Overview

Briven ("the App") is a manual, structural budgeting app for iOS — no bank connections, no CSV/OFX import, no accounts. You enter your income sources, subscriptions, and expenses yourself, and Briven turns that into a clear monthly picture.

Briven is a Privacy-First app. Your financial data is stored on your device and, optionally, synced only to your own private Apple iCloud. We run no backend server and cannot see your income, expenses, or subscriptions. Anonymous, aggregated usage analytics help us improve the app — never your financial data.

By installing or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

3. Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics

Briven uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) to understand how the app is used and to diagnose crashes.

These tools collect only anonymous, aggregated data such as:

  • App open frequency and session duration
  • Feature usage events (e.g., adding an expense, opening Reports)
  • Device type, OS version, and app version
  • Crash logs and stack traces
  • Country/region (derived from IP, not GPS)
Firebase never receives your income, expenses, subscriptions, categories, or any other financial data you enter — that data never leaves your device or your own iCloud. Analytics data is used solely to improve app stability and feature design, and is never sold or used for advertising.

For details on how Firebase handles data, please refer to the Firebase Privacy Policy.

💳

4. RevenueCat (Future Lifetime Purchase)

Briven is currently free. A future update will introduce an optional, one-time lifetime purchase (not a subscription) to unlock features such as savings goals and multi-year trend reports. When available, this purchase will be processed using RevenueCat.

RevenueCat does not collect personally identifiable information. It receives only an anonymous purchase receipt token issued by Apple's in-app purchase system — never your name, email, or payment card details. This data is used solely to verify and restore your purchase.

For details on how RevenueCat handles data, please refer to the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

5. How We Use Information

Collected information is used solely for the following purposes.

  • App Services: Tracking your income, subscriptions, and expenses, and turning them into monthly summaries and reports
  • Multi-Device Sync: Synchronizing your financial data across your own Apple devices via iCloud
  • App Lock: Verifying Face ID/Touch ID locally to protect access to the app
  • Purchases: Validating and restoring your optional lifetime purchase via RevenueCat (when available)
  • Improvement: Understanding feature usage and diagnosing crashes via Firebase to guide development

We do not sell or share your financial data or usage data with any third party for marketing purposes.

10. Security

Your financial data is stored using Apple's SwiftData framework, protected by the device's file system encryption.

When iCloud sync is enabled, data is transmitted to and stored in Apple's private CloudKit database under Apple's encryption policy, both in transit and at rest. Your data is never transmitted to our servers.

When app lock is enabled, Face ID / Touch ID authentication is handled entirely by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework and the device's Secure Enclave — your biometric data never leaves your device and Briven never has access to it.

1. 개요

Briven(이하 "앱")은 은행 연동, CSV/OFX 임포트, 계정 로그인이 전혀 없는 순수 매뉴얼 구조적 예산관리 앱입니다. 수입원, 구독, 지출을 사용자가 직접 입력하면 Briven이 이를 명확한 월별 현황으로 정리해 보여줍니다.

Briven은 프라이버시 우선 앱입니다. 사용자의 금융 데이터는 기기에 저장되며, 선택적으로 사용자 본인 소유의 Apple iCloud에만 동기화됩니다. 당사는 백엔드 서버를 운영하지 않으며 사용자의 수입, 지출, 구독 내역을 볼 수 없습니다. 익명 집계 사용 통계만 앱 개선에 활용되며, 금융 데이터는 절대 포함되지 않습니다.

앱을 설치하거나 사용함으로써 본 방침에 동의하는 것으로 간주됩니다. 동의하지 않으시면 앱 사용을 중단해 주세요.

4. RevenueCat (향후 라이프타임 구매)

Briven은 현재 무료입니다. 향후 업데이트에서 저축 목표, 다년 트렌드 리포트 등을 잠금 해제하는 선택적 라이프타임 구매(구독 아님, 1회 결제)가 추가될 예정입니다. 이 기능이 제공되면 RevenueCat을 통해 결제가 처리됩니다.

RevenueCat은 개인 식별 정보를 수집하지 않습니다. Apple의 인앱 결제 시스템이 발급하는 익명의 구매 영수증 토큰만 수신하며, 사용자의 이름, 이메일, 결제 카드 정보는 전달되지 않습니다. 해당 데이터는 구매 확인 및 복원 목적으로만 사용됩니다.

RevenueCat의 데이터 처리 방식에 대한 자세한 내용은 RevenueCat 개인정보 처리방침을 참고해 주세요.

10. セキュリティ

お客様の家計データはAppleのSwiftDataフレームワークを用いて保存され、端末のファイル システム暗号化によって保護されています。

iCloud同期を有効にしている場合、データは転送中および保存中の両方でAppleのプライベート CloudKitデータベース内でAppleの暗号化ポリシーに従い保護されます。お客様のデータが当社の サーバーに送信されることはありません。

アプリロックを使用している場合、Face ID/Touch ID認証はAppleのLocalAuthentication フレームワークと端末のSecure Enclaveによって完全に処理されます — 生体情報が端末の外に 出ることはなく、Brivenがこの情報にアクセスすることはありません。
