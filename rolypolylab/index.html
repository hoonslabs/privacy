1. Overview

Roly Poly Lab ("the App") is an idle game about breeding roly-polies (isopods), finding hidden genes and filling a Field Guide. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices.

The App has no account system. Your colony, your isopods and your Field Guide are saved only on your device. We run no server that stores your game.

By installing or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the App.

3. Advertising

The App is free and shows ads from Google AdMob.

  • Rewarded ads play only when you tap a button that says it shows an ad, in exchange for an in-game reward.
  • Interstitial ads may appear right after you start a new colony — never in your first session, at most a few times a day, and never again once you have made any purchase.
  • There are no banner ads.
All ad requests are non-personalized. The App does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use your device's advertising identifier for tracking.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), a consent message from Google is shown, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy Choices in the App.

For details on how Google uses data from apps that show its ads, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

🔥

4. Firebase Services

The App uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC).

Firebase Analytics collects Firebase's default events (such as app opens and screen views) and a small set of game events — for example starting a new colony, returning after time away, watching a rewarded ad, viewing or completing a purchase, cataloguing a morph or breeding a pair. These events contain no name, no email, no user account and no text you type.

Advertising-related consent signals for Analytics (ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization) are permanently set to denied. If you tell the App you are under 13, Analytics is turned off.

Firebase Crashlytics collects the following only when the App crashes: device model, OS version, app version, the crash location (stack trace) and its time.

For details, see Firebase Privacy.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Game progress and year of birth: stored on your device and removed when you delete the App.
  • Purchases: kept by Apple with your Apple ID; non-consumable purchases can be restored after reinstalling.
  • Advertising, analytics and crash data: kept by Google under its retention policies.
Because your game is saved only on your device, deleting the App also deletes your colony. We cannot recover it.
⚖️

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data. Since we hold no account or game data on any server, most requests concern data held by Google; you can also contact us and we will help.

US residents: We do not sell your personal information, and ads in the App are non-personalized. You can review your ad consent choice at any time under Settings → Privacy Choices.
👶

8. Children

The App is made for anyone who likes science and nature. On first launch it asks for your year of birth, once, with no answer pre-selected. The answer stays on your device.

If the answer shows you are under 13:

  • ad requests are marked as child-directed and limited to general-audience (G-rated) ads, and are always non-personalized;
  • Firebase Analytics is turned off (Crashlytics still reports crashes, which contain no personal information).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child's information was collected, please contact us so we can help.

🔒

9. Security

  • There is no account, so there are no passwords to protect.
  • Your game is never sent to our servers.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Apple; the App never sees your payment details.
