1. Overview

Chroia ("the App") is a camera app that reproduces risograph printing — multi-plate color separation, halftone dot screens, and intentional plate misregistration — live in the viewfinder and at full quality when you press the shutter. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

Chroia saves every photo you capture directly to your own iOS Photos Library, inside a dedicated "Chroia" album. We do not run any server that stores your photos, and we cannot access them.

By installing or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

3. How Your Media Is Stored

Every photo captured by Chroia is written directly to your device's iOS Photos Library, inside a dedicated "Chroia" album created by the App via Apple's Photos framework — the same system every camera app uses to save photos on iOS.

  • Photos are protected by whatever device-level security you already have configured (device passcode, Face ID / Touch ID, Photos app permissions) — Chroia adds no separate lock of its own.
  • The App also stores the name of the mechanism used (e.g. "Riso Duotone", "Offset Newsprint") in the photo's standard EXIF/TIFF metadata, so it travels with the photo when you share or export it.
  • Media is never transmitted to any server we operate. We have no ability to view, copy, or retrieve your photos.
Your photos live only in your own Photos Library. Deleting the App does not delete photos already saved there — they remain exactly where iOS Photos keeps them, under your control.
🔥

4. Firebase Services

Chroia uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) to understand app quality and stability.

Firebase Analytics collects only Firebase's default, automatically-gathered events (such as app opens and screen views). The App does not send any custom event beyond this default collection.

All advertising-related consent signals (ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization) are permanently set to denied. Chroia shows no ads, does no ad tracking, and never requests App Tracking Transparency permission.

Firebase Crashlytics collects the following only when the App crashes:

  • Device model, OS version, and app version
  • Crash location (stack trace)
  • Timestamp of the crash (UTC)
Crashlytics data contains no information that can personally identify you, and never includes your photos.

For details on how Firebase handles data, see Firebase Privacy.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Photos: Live in your iOS Photos Library, under the standard iOS Photos deletion/Recently Deleted behavior — entirely governed by iOS, not by Chroia.
  • App settings (local): Mechanism/ink preferences are stored on-device and removed when you uninstall the App.
  • Purchase records: Retained by Apple per the App Store's own policies. See Section 5.
  • Analytics & crash data: Subject to Google's data retention policies.
We hold no copy of your photos anywhere. Uninstalling the App does not remove them — they stay in your Photos Library exactly as any other photo would.
