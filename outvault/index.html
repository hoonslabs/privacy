1. Overview

OutVault: Private Camera ("the App") is a professional-grade private camera app built to keep your photos and videos completely isolated from the iOS Photos library. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

OutVault is a sandbox-first app. Every photo and video you capture is stored exclusively in the app's private sandbox — never written to iOS Photos. Your media never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it.

By installing or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

3. How Your Media Is Stored

All photos and videos captured by OutVault are written directly to the app's Application Support/assets/ directory — completely bypassing iOS Photos. The App never calls any iOS Photos library API.

  • All files are protected with NSFileProtectionComplete, Apple's highest level of on-device file encryption. Files are inaccessible while the device is locked.
  • Face ID / Passcode authentication is required to open the App.
  • Media is never transmitted to our servers.
  • When you share a file, data leaves the app only through the standard iOS share sheet under your explicit control. Pro users may strip EXIF metadata before sharing.
Your media exists only on your device (and optionally in iCloud — see Section 5) until you choose to share it. We have no access to your photos or videos.
🔥

4. Firebase Services

OutVault uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) to improve app quality and stability.

Firebase Analytics collects the following anonymous events:

  • Screen transitions and feature tap events
  • In-app interactions (e.g., photo capture, gallery view, share)
Firebase Analytics does not collect personally identifiable information and never accesses your photos or videos. All data is anonymous and used solely to improve the App. It is never sold or used for marketing purposes.

Firebase Crashlytics collects the following upon a crash:

  • Device model, OS version, and app version
  • Crash location (stack trace)
  • Timestamp of the crash (UTC)
Crashlytics data is used solely to improve app stability and contains no information that can personally identify you. Your media files are never included.

For details on how Firebase handles data, please refer to Firebase Privacy.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Photos & Videos (local): Stored in the app sandbox on your device. Permanently deleted when you delete individual assets from within the App, or when you uninstall the App.
  • Auto-Delete (Pro): Assets with an expiration date are automatically purged by the App. You control the expiration window per asset.
  • iCloud sync (future Pro feature): If CloudKit sync is enabled, uninstalling the App alone does not remove your iCloud copy. Use Settings → Reset All Data before uninstalling to fully remove iCloud data.
  • Analytics & crash data: Subject to Google's and RevenueCat's respective data retention policies.
We hold no copy of your photos or videos. Uninstalling the App (without iCloud sync) is sufficient to completely remove all media data.

9. Security

OutVault is built around security as a first principle, not an afterthought:

  • All media is stored with NSFileProtectionComplete — files are encrypted and inaccessible while the device is locked.
  • Face ID / Passcode authentication is required on every app launch and after the app is backgrounded.
  • The iOS Photos library API is never called — captured media is routed directly into the app sandbox.
  • Pro users can strip all EXIF metadata before sharing a file.
  • Your media is never transmitted to our servers under any circumstance.
