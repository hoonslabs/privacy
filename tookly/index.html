1. Overview

Tookly ("the App") is a premium medication tracker designed for people managing ADHD and chronic conditions. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Tookly is a Local-First app. Your medication data is stored on your device by default. No account or sign-up is required. If you are signed into iCloud, data may also sync via Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). Your data is never transmitted to our servers.

By installing or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use.

3. Firebase Services

Tookly uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) to improve stability and features.

Firebase Analytics collects anonymous events such as:

  • Onboarding completion
  • Notification permission grant/deny
  • Medication added (no medication name or dose is sent)
  • Dose taken / skipped / snoozed
  • CSV export started
Medication names, doses, condition tags, or health records are never sent to Firebase. All analytics events are anonymous and cannot be linked to any individual.

Firebase Crashlytics collects upon a crash: device model, OS version, app version, and stack trace. No health data is included.

See Firebase Privacy for details.
