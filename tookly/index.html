1. Overview

Tookly ("the App") is a premium medication tracker designed for people managing ADHD and chronic conditions. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Tookly is a Local-First app. Your medication data is stored on your device by default. No account or sign-up is required. If you are signed into iCloud, data may also sync via Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). Your data is never transmitted to our servers.

By installing or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use.

3. Firebase Services

Tookly uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) to improve stability and features.

Firebase Analytics collects anonymous events such as:

  • Onboarding completion
  • Notification permission grant/deny
  • HealthKit permission grant/deny
  • Medication added (no medication name or dose is sent)
  • Dose taken / skipped / snoozed
  • CSV export started
Medication names, doses, condition tags, or health records are never sent to Firebase. All analytics events are anonymous and cannot be linked to any individual.

Firebase Crashlytics collects upon a crash: device model, OS version, app version, and stack trace. No health data is included.

See Firebase Privacy for details.

🎯

4. How We Use Information

  • App Services: Medication scheduling, reminders, streak tracking, stock management
  • HealthKit: Logging taken doses to Apple Health (write-only, with your permission)
  • App Improvement: Anonymous feature usage analysis via Firebase Analytics
  • Stability: Crash diagnosis via Firebase Crashlytics

We do not sell, rent, or share your medication data with any third party.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local Data: Stored on your device and deleted when you uninstall the App.
  • iCloud Data: If iCloud sync is active, uninstalling the App alone does not remove your iCloud copy. To fully delete, go to your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage and remove Tookly data.
  • Analytics Data: Subject to Google's retention policies (Firebase).
We hold no copy of your medication data. If you do not use iCloud, uninstalling the App removes all data completely.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have the following rights:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right of Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to Restriction: Request limitation of processing
California residents (CCPA): You have the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell your data.

8. Children's Privacy

Tookly is not directed to children under 13 (US) or 16 (EU/EEA). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the App, please contact us and we will delete the relevant data promptly.

9. Security

Medication data is stored in SwiftData on iOS and protected by the device's file system encryption. Optional Face ID / Touch ID lock is available within the App.

When iCloud sync is enabled, data is protected by Apple's end-to-end encryption policies both in transit and at rest. Your data is never transmitted to our servers.

1. 개요

Tookly(이하 "앱")는 ADHD 및 만성질환을 관리하는 사용자를 위한 프리미엄 복약 트래커입니다. 본 개인정보 처리방침은 앱이 수집하는 정보, 사용 방식, 그리고 사용자의 권리를 설명합니다.

Tookly는 로컬 우선(Local-First) 앱입니다. 복약 데이터는 기본적으로 기기에 저장됩니다. 가입이나 로그인이 필요 없습니다. iCloud에 로그인한 경우, Apple iCloud(CloudKit)를 통해 동기화될 수 있습니다. 당사 서버로는 어떠한 데이터도 전송되지 않습니다.

앱을 설치하거나 사용함으로써 본 방침에 동의하는 것으로 간주됩니다.

3. Firebase 서비스

Tookly는 앱 안정성 및 기능 개선을 위해 Firebase Analytics와 Firebase Crashlytics(Google LLC)를 사용합니다.

Firebase Analytics가 수집하는 익명 이벤트:

  • 온보딩 완료
  • 알림 권한 허용/거부
  • HealthKit 권한 허용/거부
  • 약 추가 (약 이름, 용량은 전송되지 않음)
  • 복약 완료 / 건너뜀 / 스누즈
  • CSV 내보내기 시작
약 이름, 용량, 질환 태그, 건강 기록은 Firebase로 전송되지 않습니다. 모든 이벤트는 익명이며 개인을 특정할 수 없습니다.

Firebase Crashlytics는 충돌 발생 시 기기 모델, OS 버전, 앱 버전, 스택 트레이스를 수집합니다. 건강 데이터는 포함되지 않습니다.

자세한 내용은 Firebase 개인정보 보호를 참고해 주세요.

  • 앱 서비스 제공: 복약 스케줄링, 알림, 스트릭 추적, 재고 관리

  • 앱 개선: Firebase Analytics를 통한 익명 기능 사용 패턴 분석
  • 안정성 향상: Firebase Crashlytics를 통한 충돌 분석
